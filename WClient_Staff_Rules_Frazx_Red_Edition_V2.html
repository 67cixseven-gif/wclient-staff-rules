WCLIENT STAFF RULES

RECREATED BY FRAZX

01

SECTION 1: SERVER INTEGRITY & MANAGEMENT

  • [R-001] Follow Rules & Be a Good Example: Staff members must follow all normal server rules alongside staff rules. Always act mature, calm, and respectful in public channels, and do not start, join, or fuel arguments in chat or inside the team.
  • [R-002] Keep Staff Chats Private: Everything discussed in staff-only channels must stay inside those channels, and never bring regular members into hidden staff channels. Sharing staff chats, logs, or private decisions with regular members will get you demoted.
  • [R-003] Secure Your Account: You must turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your Discord account (if you dont know how go to YouTube). Never share, sell, or give your staff account or permissions to anyone else, and tell high management immediately if you think your account was hacked or compromised.
  • [R-004] No Power Abuse: Using admin powers for fun, personal arguments, or revenge is strictly banned. Impersonating high staff, owners, or developers is strictly forbidden.
  • [R-005] Don't Change Server Setup: Do not create or edit rules, channels, roles, nicknames, or server settings without approval from upper staff.
  • [R-006] Keep Profiles Clean & No Advertising: Your nickname, profile picture, and custom status must follow standard server rules. Never message members in DMs to promote other servers or products, and do not use your staff power to promote your personal YouTube, TikTok, or social media.
  • [R-007] Respect Leadership: Don't disrespect your seniors, and do not tag high staff without specific reason.

SECTION 2: CHAT MODERATION & TEXT ENFORCEMENT

  • [R-008] Hate Speech & Harassment: Delete slurs, bad hate speech, or racism immediately and mute the user. Step in immediately to stop bullying or harassment, and stop passive-aggressive or rude behavior before a big argument breaks out.
  • [R-009] Keep Chat Calm & On-Topic: Monitor chats so nothing wrong happens. Keep arguments and unnecessary toxicity out of chat, kindly tell members to move to the correct channel if they talk about the wrong topic, and mute users who cause big fights over religion or politics. No one is allowed to talk bad about owners and devs.
  • [R-010] Stop Spam & Filter Bypassing: Give a clear warning for light chat spam before giving a mute/timeout. Use bot purge commands to quickly clean flooded channels, delete messages with mass tags and timeout the spammer, warn and timeout users who keep typing in ALL CAPS, and remove massive walls of emojis, stickers, or ASCII art. Delete unreadable zalgo text or crash-formatting strings, and mute users who try to bypass bad-word filters using symbols or weird fonts.
  • [R-011] Delete Dangerous & Illegal Content: Remove unapproved links, scam sites, or suspicious links on sight and ban the user. Delete links to pirated games, movies, or illegal downloads, messages trying to sell accounts, items, or services for real cash (RMT), and fake giveaway posts or fake server updates posted by members.
  • [R-012] Zero NSFW & No Doxxing: Delete explicit pictures, videos, or 18+ media instantly and permanently ban the sender. Instantly delete private real-life info (names, addresses, IPs) and ban the user forever.
  • [R-013] Self-Harm Prevention: Delete mentions of self-harm or suicide right away and give crisis helpline info.
  • [R-014] Block Unsafe Members: Ban/mute members if they DM you bad things.
  • [R-015] Be Loyal & Report Abuse: Don't share external exploits; be loyal to WClient. Report a high staff member if he abused you or a member.
02

SECTION 3: PUNISHMENT EXECUTION & PROTOCOLS

  • [R-016] Follow Punishment Steps: Use the correct order: Friendly Warning → Official Warning → Timeout → Kick → Ban. Skip warnings and ban immediately for extreme breaking of rules (doxxing, raids, NSFW, malware).
  • [R-017] Ban Alts & Clean Up Spammers: Immediately ban alternate accounts made to bypass active mutes, warnings, or bans. Delete 24 hours of messages when banning spam bots, raiders, or hack accounts.
  • [R-018] Punish Fairly: Always check a user's past warnings (/warnings or /logs) before choosing a punishment. Punish only the specific person who broke the rule, not the whole channel, and look closely at reported screenshots to make sure they are not edited or photoshopped. Never punish someone just because you are angry at them. Set fair timeout lengths based on warning history (e.g., 1 hour, 12 hours, 24 hours, 7 days) and make sure timeouts stop users from both texting in chat and talking in VC.
  • [R-019] Use Main Bots & Log Everything: Execute all moderation actions through the primary bot to keep logs accurate. Never give "secret" punishments without using the bot to record them properly. If you kick or ban someone manually through Discord, post the reason in logs right away.
  • [R-020] Add Reasons & Save Proof: Write a clear, accurate, professional reason every time you run a punishment command; do not write jokes, insults, or personal opinions in it. Always post screenshots, message links, or screen recordings in the staff proof channel, and record audio/video proof before punishing members for voice channel rule breaking. Blur out private phone numbers or personal emails before posting proof in logs.
  • [R-021] Protect Logs & Other Staff's Actions: Do not clear or delete official warning logs without high management approval. Never remove another staff member's mute or warning without talking to them first, and you cannot unban a user unless the staff member who banned them or a Lead says yes. Never give joke warnings, fake mutes, or fake bans to regular members.
03

SECTION 4: TICKETING & USER SUPPORT

  • [R-022] Answer Fast & Stay Professional: Try to answer new support tickets within 30 minutes and greet every user politely. Stay calm and polite even if the player is angry, and keep ticket chat focused only on the user's issue, not casual chatting. Warn users who get rude in tickets; if they keep being mean, close the ticket.
  • [R-023] Closing Tickets: Give a clear answer or quick summary and ask "Is there anything else I can help you with?" before closing. Never close a ticket while the user is still actively explaining their problem, and close tickets if the user hasn't replied for 24 hours after your last message. Download important evidence pictures or videos before deleting the ticket channel.
  • [R-024] Pass Up Big Issues: Send hard bug reports, payment questions, or staff complaints to higher management. Never promise refunds or unbans without asking high management first, and tell them if someone opens fake or troll tickets repeatedly.

SECTION 5: COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE & USER HELPING

  • [R-025] Help Players Clearly: Offer clear help when members are confused about server systems or roles, and guide new members on how to pick roles and find main channels. Point members to the FAQ or rules channel before writing long explanations, explain technical steps in simple, easy words, and use simple, clear English or translation bots for players who speak other languages. Ask for error screenshots or video clips for technical issues, and send members to official links for client downloads or game news.
  • [R-026] Be Kind & Pass Bugs to Devs: Never laugh at or make fun of members who ask easy or repetitive questions, and thank and encourage helpful members who accurately answer support questions. Collect bug details from players and send clean reports to server developers.
04

SECTION 6: FEEDBACK, SUGGESTIONS & REVIEW

  • [R-027] Accept Feedback & Credit: Listen to member feedback and criticism without getting angry or defensive. The first staff member to actively help the user gets the feedback credit; if a member gives feedback to 2 staff members, credit goes to the first helper or whoever provided the most assistance.
  • [R-028] Handle Suggestions: Keep suggestion channels for real ideas; delete troll or joke posts. Group player suggestions and bug reports together and forward them to upper staff, track top requested features for the development team, and tell high management if many members are upset about a new server update or rule. If a popular suggestion gets denied, give players a simple, logical reason why.
  • [R-029] Discuss Ideas in Staff Chat: Share your personal ideas for the server in private staff channels during team reviews. Never fight with members in public suggestion threads; discuss them in staff chat. Watch player reactions carefully for 48 hours after launching big updates or rule changes.

SECTION 7: CONFLICT RESOLUTION & DE-ESCALATION

  • [R-030] De-escalate Fights: Move fighting players out of main chat and into tickets or private messages, and turn on slowmode (5s to 30s) if general chat moves too fast during arguments. Lower chat tension by smoothly changing the topic to something casual. Stay completely calm even if a rude user attacks you verbally; never argue back.
  • [R-031] Stay Neutral in Fights: Listen to both people involved in an argument before punishing anyone, and do not pick favorites during public player fights.
  • [R-032] Cool Off Fairly: Give equal cooling-off timeouts to both users if they refuse to stop arguing. Never use mutes or purges to win an argument or quiet someone you disagree with.
05

SECTION 8: STAFF ACTIVITY, ATTENDANCE & LEAVE

  • [R-033] Daily Quota & Active Support: Send at least 200 messages daily, actively assist anyone who needs help, and help at least 2 members per day (no proof required).
  • [R-034] Don't Vanish Without Notice: Going offline for days without a leave request will lead to demotion for inactivity.
  • [R-035] Read & Confirm Announcements: Check and read internal staff update channels at least once every 24 hours, and add the required reaction emoji on staff posts to confirm you read them.

SECTION 9: ROLE PERMISSIONS & BOT USAGE

  • [R-036] Protect Permissions: Never assign temporary admin permissions to normal users for any reason. Do not change the hierarchy of server roles or channel-specific permission locks set up by owners without lead approval.
  • [R-037] Use Bots Correctly: Run moderation bot commands only in designated bot-command channels, and never test bot features or mess with settings in general public chat. Never use force-nickname commands to joke around or embarrass members, and do not run bot commands that cause big role mentions without lead permission.
  • [R-038] Report Bugs & Dead Bots: Tell owners immediately if you find a server bug, exploit, or broken permissions, and tell tech leads immediately if a moderation bot goes offline or stops working.
06

SECTION 10: SECURITY & OFF-TOPIC CONTENT

  • [R-039] Ban Malware & Report Severe Offenses: Instantly ban accounts sharing viruses, grabbers, or dangerous files, and clear chat. Delete invite links to illegal, unsafe, or malicious external servers, and report severe bot-nets or illegal content to Discord Trust & Safety.
  • [R-040] Check Profiles & Hidden Hate: Make sure user avatars, banners, statuses, and names follow server rules. Act fast on hidden meanings, bypass language, or toxic disguises.

SECTION 11: PROFESSIONAL ETHICS & HIERARCHY

  • [R-041] Stay Neutral & Fair: Keep personal real-world politics or opinions out of your work while on duty. Treat every member fairly, whether they are a VIP booster, rich player, or beginner, and do not let personal feelings affect your punishments. Never accept money, items, or gifts in exchange for unbans or staff favors.
  • [R-042] Be Honest & Own Your Mistakes: Never lie about your staff activity logs or fake your work. Fake logs cause instant demotion. If you make a mistake in moderation, admit it honestly and fix it with senior staff.
  • [R-043] Protect Privacy & Keep Fights Private: Never share private user support tickets or details outside the staff team, and never tell anyone who submitted a report or ticket against them. Keep internal team disagreements completely out of public chat channels and talk about them in private staff chats.
  • [R-044] Active Service or Resign: Trial staff members are not allowed to request or take a leave of absence. Staff who cannot remain active must resign officially by posting in the leave/Resignation channel first; inactive staff will be demoted over time to prevent wasting server time.
  • [R-045] Management Control: Management keeps all rights to change roles, edit permissions, or demote staff as needed.

SECTION 12: STAFF REWARDS & INCENTIVES

  • [R-046] Weekly Staff Salary: Every active staff member receives 15,000 coins every week.
  • [R-047] Daily Top Chatter Bonus: The top #1 staff member with the most daily messages earns 5,000 coins.
  • [R-048] Staff of the Week: The staff member who receives the most positive feedback in a week wins the @best staff of the week role, 50,000 to 100,000 coins, and 7 days of free verification.

SECTION 13: ROLE PROMOTIONS & HIERARCHY

  • [R-049] How Promotions Work: Your score for a promotion is based on these 5 things:
    • • 35% Activity: How active you are in chat and tickets every day.
    • • 20% Clean Record: Not breaking any staff rules or getting strikes.
    • • 20% Helping: How much you help players and other staff.
    • • 15% Feedbacks: Getting good reviews from players.
    • • 15% Managing: Showing good control and helping manage the server.

  • [R-050] Staff Promotion Steps: You must move up through ranks in this exact order:
Trial staff -> helper -> mod -> head mod -> admin -> senior admin -> manager -> senior manager -> head of staff -> supreme -> honored one

  • [R-051] Special Side Role (W Lawyer): The @W Lawyer is a special side role given only to staff who never break rules and can fairly solve fights between staff members.
