WClient Staff Rules

Frazx Red Edition V2

Core Standards

  • Maintain professionalism at all times.
  • Be respectful and objective in communications.
  • Prioritize safety, fairness, and clarity.
  • Use authority with accountability and restraint.

Behavior

  • Do not harass, bait, or target users.
  • Never abuse privileges or exploit loopholes.
  • Encourage cooperation and calm conflict resolution.
  • Escalate serious issues to the proper authority.

Team Conduct

  • Keep actions aligned with community values.
  • Share information clearly and honestly.
  • Document decisions when consequences apply.
  • Support the mission without favoritism.

Operational Rules

  • All staff members must act in service of fairness, order, and public trust.
  • Use tools, moderation actions, and reports responsibly and with evidence.
  • Maintain accurate records and do not falsify outcomes or discussions.
  • Any harmful conduct, intimidation, or abuse of authority is grounds for review.
  • Staff must remain transparent when a conflict of interest, error, or misconduct is identified.
